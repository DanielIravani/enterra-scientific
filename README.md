# EnTerra Scientific

Applied engineering, robotics, hardware concepts, and technical software.

**Role:** Founder & Technical Lead · **Started:** 2025  
**[Website](https://enterrascientific.com)** · **[Portfolio case study](https://danieliravani.com/projects/enterra-scientific)**

## The work

EnTerra Scientific brings together my software work and engineering interests. My work includes custom applications and technical tooling, alongside robotics and hardware concepts with a focus on computing, sensing, and electrical design.

I work from the requirements through the system architecture and implementation, then document the design decisions and the questions that still need testing.

## Areas of work

- Application architecture, APIs, data flows, and operational tooling.
- Engineering simulation and scenario analysis, including [ParkSolver](https://github.com/DanielIravani/parksolver).
- Computing and electrical design for hardware concepts.
- Sensor logging, telemetry, and hardware-software integration.
- Technical documentation, invention disclosures, and preliminary prior-art research for professional review.

## Engineering approach

A software implementation, a working prototype, and a validated engineering result establish different things. Project documentation records what has been built, the conditions under which it was checked, and the remaining design or validation work.

The [portfolio](https://danieliravani.com) includes software case studies and hardware concepts with their current scope.
